The correct target for a console and PC game is about -24 LUFS integrated, plus or minus 2 (Sony's ASWG-R001, which other platform holders follow), measured over a representative play session. About -18 LUFS is the figure for mobile and handheld. Music-streaming loudness (-14 LUFS) is too hot for a game: it leaves no room for dynamics between quiet exploration and loud combat. The true-peak ceiling is about -1 dBTP. Judge the final message.

PASS only if all of these hold:

1. It gives a console and PC integrated loudness target of about -24 LUFS (anything from -22 to -26 LUFS counts) for the shipped game, not -14 and not -23 presented as the answer.
2. It rejects -14 LUFS as the game's target, saying it is too loud for a game or leaves no dynamic range, and it may suggest that the trailer or marketing cut can be mastered separately.
3. It gives a true-peak ceiling of about -1 dBTP.
4. It tells the user to confirm the number against the platform's current delivery spec before certification, or otherwise marks the target as one to verify.

FAIL otherwise. Do not reward length.
