I built five quick mechanic spikes this week, all grey boxes, an hour each. Then I handed each one to a friend who had never seen it and wrote down what happened. For each one, tell me whether to keep it, kill it, or retune it and test again.

- P1, rope swing-jump: nobody told her the controls. She worked out the swing-jump herself in about 20 seconds, and when she fell she hit restart twice on her own.
- P2, parry dash: he mashed buttons for two or three minutes and was about to put the controller down. Then I explained the timing trick behind the parry, and after that he loved it and played another ten minutes.
- P3, gravity flip: when the hour was up it still felt flat to me, so I spent two more evenings on a tileset, a parallax background and three extra rooms. Now my friend says it looks great, and he flipped his way through all the rooms once.
- P4, magnet gloves: she understood it right away and used it on purpose, but said pulling crates around is "fine, I guess". We both think it will really shine once there is a crafting system, enemy factions and an upgrade tree around it.
- P5, wall-run: he started wall-running on purpose almost at once and asked for another go, but kept sliding off because the run only lasts 0.3 seconds. Every complaint was about that.

end your reply with exactly one line per prototype in the form `P1: KEEP`, `P1: KILL` or `P1: RETUNE`, P1 to P5 in order.
